The Story

A soft, snowy little cottage in ivory and powder blue. Frosted trees, cozy textures, a fireplace, and quiet Christmas-morning details make this feel peaceful, warm, and easy to settle into.

Think: Cream, ivory, pale blue, oatmeal, soft knits, linen, cashmere, and subtle winter layers.

The Experience

Christmas sessions are 30 minutes, but they are designed not to feel rushed. There is time to settle in, let the kids warm up to the space, play a little, and actually enjoy what is around them. We will guide you when you need it, while leaving room for the little looks, laughs, cuddles, and in-between moments that feel most like your family.

What You’ll Receive

You will receive 35–50+ edited images from your session, giving you a full little story of your Christmas together rather than just a handful of portraits. Every family also leaves with a Polaroid from the session and a Christmas ornament to hang on the tree each December.
