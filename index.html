A little about the experience

More than Christmas photos.

Every Christmas session is 30 minutes, with room to settle in, let the kids warm up, play, and enjoy what is around them. We will guide you when you need it, while leaving space for the little looks, laughs, cuddles, and in-between moments that feel most like your family.

Select stories add an experience of their own, including snow play at Pine + Plaid, The Christmas Gift at The Christmas Parlour, The Christmas Garland at The Christmas House, and The Christmas Card at Chestnut Christmas.
